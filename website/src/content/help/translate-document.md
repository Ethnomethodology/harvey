Convert your documents into other languages directly within the Data tab.

*   **Supported Formats:** Works with Lexical JSON, Markdown, and plain text files.
*   **Batch Translation:** Select multiple documents in the Data list to translate them in a single operation.
*   **Output:** Translated documents are saved as new assets in your project, linked to the originals.
