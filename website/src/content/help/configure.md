### Configure

The Configure screen allows you to manage system-level settings, hardware acceleration, and AI models.

*   **[Application](#config-app)**: Theme and download locations.
*   **[Transcription Engine](#config-transcription)**: Whisper model management.
*   **[Diarization Engine](#config-diarization)**: Speaker identification setup.
*   **[Translation Engine](#config-translation)**: Local translation models.
*   **[Advanced Settings](#config-advanced)**: Developer tools and logs.
