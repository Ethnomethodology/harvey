The Configure screen allows you to manage system-level settings, hardware acceleration, and AI models.

*   **[Application](/help/config-app)**: Theme and download locations.
*   **[Transcription Engine](/help/config-transcription)**: Whisper model management.
*   **[Diarization Engine](/help/config-diarization)**: Speaker identification setup.
*   **[Translation Engine](/help/config-translation)**: Local translation models.
*   **[Advanced Settings](/help/config-advanced)**: Developer tools and logs.
