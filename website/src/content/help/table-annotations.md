### Table Annotations

Annotate structured data in CSV or Excel tables.

*   **Cell Tagging:** Apply tags to individual cells or entire rows.
*   **Data Interpretation:** Add comments to explain specific data points or outliers.
*   **Visual Cues:** Color-code rows based on applied tags for quick visual analysis.
