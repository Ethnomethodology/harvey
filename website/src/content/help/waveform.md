The interactive waveform provides a visual representation of your audio and segment timing.

*   **Interactive Handles:** In the vertical waveform view, you can drag the top and bottom handles of any segment block to precisely adjust its timing.
*   **Visual Cues:** See pauses and loud sections at a glance, helping you identify speaker turns and natural segment boundaries.
*   **Navigation:** Click anywhere on the waveform to seek. The red playhead shows the current position.
*   **Zooming:** Use the zoom controls or `Ctrl/Cmd + Scroll` to inspect fine audio details.
