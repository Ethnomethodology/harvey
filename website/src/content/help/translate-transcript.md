Translate your generated transcripts while preserving all metadata.

*   **Structural Preservation:** Translation maintains exact timestamps and speaker associations.
*   **Workflow:** Open a transcript in the Transcription tab, click the Translate button, and select your target language.
*   **Review:** Toggle between original and translated versions to verify accuracy.
