### Export

Share your work by exporting transcripts in various formats.

*   **Standard Formats:** Export to Microsoft Word (.docx), Markdown (.md), or Plain Text (.txt).
*   **Subtitles:** Generate timed subtitle files in SRT, VTT, or ASS formats.
*   **Options:** Choose which elements to include, such as speaker names and timestamps.
