### Image Annotations

Work with visual evidence by annotating image files.

*   **Bounding Boxes:** Draw rectangles around specific areas of interest in an image.
*   **Geometric Shapes:** Use circles, lines, or polygons for precise visual marking.
*   **Annotorious:** Harvey integrates with powerful image annotation libraries to provide professional-grade tools.
