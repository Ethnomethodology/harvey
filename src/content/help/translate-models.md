Harvey uses specialized AI models for offline translation.

*   **Helsinki-NLP:** Highly efficient models for specific language pairs.
*   **NLLB (No Language Left Behind):** Facebook's universal model for translating between 200+ languages.
*   **Management:** Use the Translation Engine settings in the Configure screen to download and optimize these models for your CPU.
