Interpretation of dialogue and social interaction.

*   **Interactional Tagging:** Apply specialized tags to capture conversational patterns.
*   **Speaker Context:** Annotate based on who is speaking and the social dynamics at play.
*   **Synchronization:** Transcript annotations are linked to the source media for immediate verification.
