### Editing

Refine your transcripts with powerful, time-aware editing tools.

*   **Edit Mode:** Toggle edit mode using the toolbar button or `Ctrl/Cmd + E`.
*   **Gapless Continuity:** When you adjust the start or end time of a segment, the adjacent segments are automatically updated to prevent gaps or overlaps, ensuring a perfect timeline.
*   **Standardized Layout:** Each segment uses a clear two-row layout: Metadata (Speaker & Time) at the top, and formatted text below for maximum clarity.
*   **Formatting:** Apply bold, italic, or underline styles to emphasize specific parts of the text.
