Annotation is the core process of adding analytical value to your project data. Harvey supports a wide range of annotation types across different media and document formats.

*   **[Media Annotations](/help/media-annotations)**: Annotating audio and video segments.
*   **[Document Annotations](/help/document-annotations)**: Working with text documents.
*   **[PDF Annotations](/help/pdf-annotations)**: Visual highlights on PDFs.
*   **[Image Annotations](/help/image-annotations)**: Geometric bounding boxes.
*   **[Table Annotations](/help/table-annotations)**: Categorizing tabular data.
*   **[Transcript Annotations](/help/transcript-annotations)**: Interpreting dialogue.
*   **[Highlights Panel](/help/highlights-panel)**: Reviewing all annotations.
