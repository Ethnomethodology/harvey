A Harvey Project is a self-contained folder structure. Moving this folder moves your entire research environment.

```
Project Folder/
├── ProjectName.harvey (JSON Manifest)
└── harvey_files/
    ├── Audios/ (Imported audio files)
    ├── Videos/ (Imported video files)
    ├── Documents/ (JSON/PDF/MD)
    ├── Images/ (Imported images)
    ├── Tables/ (CSV/XLSX files)
    └── Transcripts/ (Imported transcripts)
```
