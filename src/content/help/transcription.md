# Transcription

Harvey uses OpenAI's Whisper models running locally on your machine.

## Selecting a Model

Go to **Settings > Models**.
Choose between `tiny`, `base`, `small`, `medium`, or `large`.
Larger models are more accurate but slower.

## Diarization

Enable **Speaker Identification** to automatically label speakers (e.g., Speaker A, Speaker B).
