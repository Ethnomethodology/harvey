### Media Player

The integrated media player is optimized for transcription workflows.

*   **Synchronization:** Clicking any text segment jumps the player to that exact moment in the audio/video.
*   **Playback Speed:** Adjust speed from 0.5x to 2.0x to match your typing pace.
*   **Reliable Trimming:** Use the trim tool to extract clips from your media assets. The process is optimized to handle large files reliably.
