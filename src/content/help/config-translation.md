### Translation Engine

Local translation is handled by **CTranslate2** using **Helsinki-NLP** and **NLLB** models.

*   **Language Pairs:** Download specific models for the languages you need to translate (e.g., French to English, Spanish to English).
*   **Optimization:** Models are automatically optimized for high-speed CPU inference during the download process.
