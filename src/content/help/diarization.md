Automatically identify and label different speakers in an audio recording.

*   **Speaker Labels:** AI assigns consistent labels (e.g., Speaker 1, Speaker 2) across the transcript.
*   **Renaming:** Double-click any speaker label to rename it throughout the entire document.
*   **Configuration:** Requires the Pyannote diarization model, which can be enabled in the Configure screen.
